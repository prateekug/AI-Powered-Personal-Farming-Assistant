About this Disease

Leaf rust is a fungal disease that causes orange-brown pustules on leaf surfaces. It can significantly reduce crop yield if not treated early.

Impact on Crop

  • Reduced photosynthesis capacity
  • Premature leaf death
  • Yield reduction up to 40%
  • Lower grain quality

Recommended Treatments

💊
Fungicide Application

Apply Propiconazole (1ml/liter) or Tebuconazole (1ml/liter) as foliar spray. Repeat after 15 days if necessary.

🌱
Fertilizer Recommendation

Apply Potassium (K) fertilizer to strengthen plant resistance. Use SOP (Sulphate of Potash) at 50kg/acre.

💧
Water Management

Avoid overhead irrigation. Use drip irrigation to keep foliage dry and prevent fungal spread.

Prevention Tips

  • Plant resistant varieties
  • Crop rotation with non-host crops
  • Remove infected plant debris
  • Maintain proper plant spacing
  • Monitor crops regularly
